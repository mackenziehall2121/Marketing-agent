# Reeves: Week 1 Schedule

**Hours:** about 5 per weekday (25 total). The weekend is for rest and an optional LinkedIn post.
**Week 1 goals:**
- Sending setup started and warming up
- 50 hand-picked prospects emailed personally
- First 200-contact YAMM batch ready
- 10 partners contacted
- Founding offer decided
- Checklist lead magnet drafted

Every day, with Claude: paste in what you're working on (prospect rows, replies, drafts) and ask for help. The business profile and playbook are already loaded in this repo.

---

## Every day (first 45 minutes, before anything else)
- **Inbox (30 min):** answer every reply from prospects within 24 hours. Say yes to every Fit Check request and put it on your list.
- **Warm-up (10 min):** from each new sending inbox, send and reply to a few normal emails with people you know, or check that your warm-up tool is running.
- **Question log (5 min):** add every question a prospect asked to one running doc. That doc becomes your content, emails and FAQ.

## What NOT to do this week
- **Don't send YAMM batches from the new domain yet.** It needs 2–3 weeks of warm-up. Your 50 personal emails are this week's outreach.
- **Don't upload the list to Mailchimp or a similar newsletter tool.**
- **Don't make up proof.** No invented results, testimonials or comparisons.
- **Don't write the founder story yet.** It's on hold until you're ready.

## If a prospect says yes
1. Run their Fit Check in Reeves the same day.
2. Send the results with a short note: "Here are 5 grants we looked at for [Org]. Want me to set this up so it runs for you? Here's a 7-day trial: https://getreeves.ai/signup.html, or I'm happy to do a 15-minute call."
3. Log it on the scoreboard.
