# Reeves Intelligence: 90-Day Marketing Plan

**Starting point:** pre-revenue, a 20,000-contact researched (cold) nonprofit list, $0 ad budget, 25+ founder hours/week, three tiers (Core $2,388/yr · Pro $4,788/yr · Max $10,788/yr), 7-day free trial, and a walkthrough demo.

**The 90-day goal:** get the first paying customers, learn the real conversion numbers, and build the channels that can reach **$1M in annual recurring revenue (ARR)**.

---

## 1. The honest math

$1M ARR ≈ **286 customers** at an assumed average of $3,500/yr (mostly Core, some Pro and Max).

Cold email alone, over about 45 sending days (after 2–3 weeks of inbox warm-up):

| Scenario | Contacts reached | Positive replies | Trials (40% of replies) | Paying (25% of trials) | ARR at $3,500 avg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base: 5 inboxes × 40/day, 2% reply | 9,000 | ~180 | ~72 | **~18** | **~$63k** |
| Strong: 10 inboxes × 45/day, 4% reply | 20,000 (whole list) | ~800 | ~320 | **~80** | **~$280k** |

**These rates are assumptions, not proof.** Replace them with real numbers after the first 2–3 batches.

**Conclusion:** $1M ARR in 90 days from cold email to one list is very unlikely, and a plan that promised it would be lying to you. Even the strong scenario falls short of $1M, and the list can only be emailed once. To close the gap you need channels that reach **many nonprofits at once** (partners) and **bigger deals** (Max tier). This plan builds both.

**90-day targets:**
- **Floor:** 20 paying customers, about $60k ARR, and a proven reply → trial → paid funnel
- **Target:** 50 customers, about $175k ARR, at least one partner channel live
- **Stretch:** 80+ customers, $280k+ ARR, and a clear path to $1M ARR within about 12 months

## 2. The five engines

### Engine 1: Cold email to the researched list (the main engine)
Runs on YAMM with `outreach/cold-sequence-v1.md`.
- Separate sending domain, 5 Workspace inboxes to start, and 10 once numbers justify it
- Batches of about 200 per tab, best-fit segments first: small, grant-dependent organizations, contacts who are executive directors or development staff
- A/B test email 1A (demo link) against 1B (no link, "want me to send it?")
- **Bottleneck: your time.** Every reply needs a fast, personal response, and 800 replies won't fit into 25 hours a week. Use Reeves itself to produce Fit Checks quickly, keep reply templates, and plan to add hours or a part-time helper by month 2 if replies come in

### Engine 2: Partners (the only fast way to reach many organizations with $0 in ads)
One partner can put Reeves in front of hundreds of nonprofits at once. Targets:
- **State nonprofit associations** in West Virginia and California: member webinars, newsletter features, member discounts
- **Community foundations** in your two regions: they want their grantees to win more outside funding
- **Freelance grant writers and grant consultants:** each serves many clients. They're natural Pro or Max users, and a referral commission can turn them into a sales force
- **Fiscal sponsors and nonprofit capacity-building programs**

Offer partners a free **live workshop for their members** on "How to tell if a grant is worth applying for, before you write a word." Teach it genuinely (give away your best stuff) and show Reeves at the end.

### Engine 3: Bigger deals (Max tier)
Ninety-three Max customers equals $1M ARR on its own. Build a separate, smaller list of **larger Los Angeles nonprofits** with development teams of 5–15 people. Approach them with personal 1:1 outreach and a live demo instead of mail merge, since the high-touch onboarding is part of what they're paying for.

### Engine 4: Build your own opted-in list
The cold list can only be emailed once. Your own list keeps working for years.
- Put a lead magnet on getreeves.ai: **"The Grant Fit Checklist: know whether a grant is worth applying for before you write a word."** Use a simple squeeze page: headline, one sentence, an email field, nothing else
- Ask everyone who replies, takes a trial or attends a workshop whether they'd like to join
- After signup: deliver the checklist, then send 3–4 warm-up emails with your best advice

### Engine 5: Founder content on LinkedIn ($0)
Executive directors and grant writers are on LinkedIn. Post 3 times a week, each post answering one real question prospects ask you (keep a running log of those questions). Every post points to the Grant Fit Checklist.

## 3. The founding-member offer (decide before week 3)
You can't show testimonials yet, so be honest and make the risk small:
> "Reeves is new. We're looking for our first [25] founding nonprofits. You get [founding price locked for life / free high-touch onboarding / X months free on annual], and in return we ask for honest feedback."

- **A guarantee:** [e.g. 30-day money-back on annual plans], to decide
- **Push annual plans:** they bring cash in upfront, and founding pricing is a good reason to prepay

## 4. Week-by-week

### Month 1: foundation and first customers
**Week 1**
- Register the sending domain, create the inboxes, set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and start warm-up
- Verify the first 2,000 addresses and fill in `MissionLine` for the first 1,000 (Claude can help in bulk)
- Decide the founding offer and guarantee
- Pick the **50 best-fit prospects** and email them 1:1 from your normal inbox. This is personal email, not a mail merge, so no warm-up is needed

**Week 2**
- Build the Grant Fit Checklist and its squeeze page
- List 20 partner targets (associations, foundations, grant consultants) and reach out to the first 10
- Write the trial welcome emails. Their job is to get each user to see Reeves assess a real grant **on day 1**
- Start LinkedIn posts, 3 a week

**Weeks 3–4**
- Start YAMM batches at 20–30/day per inbox and ramp up
- Do Fit Checks within 24 hours of every "yes"
- **Aim for 5 paying customers by day 30**
- Book the first partner workshop

### Month 2: scale what works
- Get YAMM to full volume and pick the winner of email 1A vs. 1B
- Run 1–2 partner workshops
- Start Max-tier 1:1 outreach to larger Los Angeles nonprofits
- Collect **real** feedback from founding customers, and ask permission before quoting anyone
- Add a referral ask: "Know another organization that would find this useful?"
- Add hours or help if replies exceed your capacity
- **Aim for 20 customers by day 60**

### Month 3: double down
- Put more volume into the best segment and region; add inboxes if the numbers justify it
- Write the first real case study, with the customer's permission and real numbers only
- Start regular emails to your opted-in list, alternating useful emails with offers
- Run the first offer week (open Wednesday, close Sunday) to trial users who haven't bought and to your opted-in list
- Sign at least one ongoing partner (a referral agreement or member discount)
- **Aim for 50 customers by day 90**, then plan the next 90 days from real numbers

## 5. Weekly scoreboard (track every Friday)

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Emails sent | Are you hitting volume? |
| Bounce rate (keep under 3%) | List quality; protects your domain |
| Positive replies and reply rate | Is the message working? |
| Fit Checks and demos delivered | Your time bottleneck |
| Trials started | Top of the paid funnel |
| Trial → paid rate | Is the product proving itself in 7 days? |
| New customers, by tier | Revenue |
| ARR | The number that matters |
| Partner conversations and workshops | The channel that reaches many organizations at once |
| Opted-in list size | The asset you own |

---

## 6. Budget
- **Ads:** $0, per plan
- **Tools:** a small monthly spend is unavoidable: sending domain, Google Workspace inboxes, a YAMM paid plan, and email verification. Roughly $100–$250/month to start; check current pricing
- **Once there's revenue:** reinvest in more inboxes, part-time help with replies, and eventually retargeting ads to people who visited the site or watched the demo

---

## 7. Rules that don't bend
- Never fabricate testimonials, numbers or competitor claims (see `business/reeves-intelligence.md`)
- The founder story stays on hold until the founder shares it
- Stop emailing anyone who opts out, everywhere and permanently
- Judge by replies and clicks, not opens
