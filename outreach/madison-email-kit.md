## Rules for every email
- Never invent numbers, results, testimonials or competitor claims.
- Anyone who says no or unsubscribes is never emailed again.
- Every "yes" gets its Fit Check within 2 business days.
- Add every question someone asks to the question log. New questions become new replies in this kit.
