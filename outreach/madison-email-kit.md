# Part 2: Phone

## 4. Call answered
*A 20–30 second introduction and one question about their grant process.*

> "Hi [Name], this is Madison with Reeves Intelligence. I'll be quick. We work with nonprofit teams on grant research, specifically the part where you figure out which grants actually fit your mission and eligibility before anyone spends time on an application.
>
> Can I ask you one question? When a grant looks promising, how does your team decide whether it's worth pursuing?"

**Then listen.** Depending on the answer:
- **They describe a slow or manual process:** "That's exactly what we help with. Would a 20-minute walkthrough be worth it, using grants relevant to [Org]?" Book the time on the call.
- **They're busy:** "Totally understand. Can I email you a short overview, and you look when it's convenient?" → go to #8.
- **They're not interested:** "No problem at all, thanks for your time." → go to #25 if they ask not to be called again.

## 5. Voicemail
*A brief message pointing them to Madison's email.*

> "Hi [Name], this is Madison with Reeves Intelligence. I'm reaching out because we help nonprofit teams figure out which grants actually fit their mission and eligibility, and keep the good ones organized. I'm sending you a short email now with the details. You can reply there, or reach me at [PHONE]. Thanks, [Name]!"

**Then:** send #8 (or email 1, if they haven't had it yet) within the hour.

## 6. Gatekeeper answers
*A request for the person responsible for grant research or development.*

> "Hi, this is Madison with Reeves Intelligence. I'm hoping to speak with whoever handles grant research or development for [Org]. Who would that be?"

- **If they give a name:** "Thank you! Is [Name] available, or is there a better time to reach them? And what's the best email for them?"
- **If they ask what it's about:** "We help nonprofits find grants and check whether they fit the organization's mission and eligibility. I just want to see if it could save their team some time."
- **If they offer voicemail:** leave #5.

Always thank the gatekeeper by name. They decide whether you get through next time.
