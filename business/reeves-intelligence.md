# Reeves Intelligence: Business Profile

Read this before doing any marketing work for Reeves. It holds the facts the founder has confirmed, the claims we are not allowed to make, and the questions still open.

## What it is
Reeves Intelligence helps nonprofits **find grant opportunities, assess whether they fit their mission and eligibility, and keep the strongest prospects organized**. It works alongside nonprofit staff. Reeves makes the research and tracking easier, and **the people who know the organization decide which grants to pursue**.

## Who it's for
Nonprofit teams that seek grant funding, especially:
- Executive directors
- Development staff
- Grant writers
- Small teams where one person handles several roles (the sharpest target: they feel the time cost most)

## The problem (before)
Finding grant listings is easy. Working out which ones the organization actually qualifies for and should spend time on takes hours of research. For each promising listing, staff have to check whether the funder supports their kind of work, serves their area, and is worth an application. Prospects end up scattered across spreadsheets, inboxes and memory.

## The result (after)
- A clearer view of which grants are worth pursuing
- One place where promising opportunities stay organized
- Less time spent sorting through poor fits
- More confidence about where to focus
- The team still makes the call. Reeves supports their judgment.

## The feeling we sell
Having time for the grants that matter and confidence that the time is well spent, instead of the dread of another afternoon lost to listings they don't qualify for. Every step ends in more funding for the mission they got into this work to serve.

Prospects currently rely on grant databases, consultants, spreadsheets, or their own manual research.
