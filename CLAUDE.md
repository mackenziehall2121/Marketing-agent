# Marketing-agent

Marketing workspace for **Reeves Intelligence** (getreeves.ai), a grant-research tool for nonprofits.

Before any marketing work:
1. Read `business/reeves-intelligence.md` for the business facts, audience, and the claims we must not make.
2. Use the `jaredrhod-marketing` skill (`.claude/skills/jaredrhod-marketing/`) for principles and the matching playbook.

Hard rule: Reeves has no customers or results yet. Never fabricate testimonials, numbers, or competitor claims.
